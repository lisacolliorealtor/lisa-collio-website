Goshen, Indiana School District

Most homes in Goshen fall under a single district, but not all of them — and the exceptions are easy to miss.

Goshen Community Schools

The primary district, with nine schools serving roughly 6,200 to 6,500 students: Goshen High School (home of the RedHawks), Goshen Junior High, Goshen Intermediate, Chandler Innovation Academy, and five elementary schools. Private and parochial options are also available in the area, and Goshen College serves the community at the post-secondary level.

Where it gets more complicated

Small portions of the city fall within Middlebury Community Schools, Concord Community Schools, or WaNee Community Schools instead of Goshen Community Schools. A Goshen mailing address does not guarantee a Goshen Community Schools assignment — the boundaries follow district lines, not city limits.

How to confirm which district applies

The only reliable method is to confirm directly with the applicable district's enrollment office before writing an offer — for both attendance assignment and, separately, bus eligibility, since that isn't guaranteed for every address even within an assigned district. Lisa can help identify which boundary a specific address falls in and point you to the right office, but the confirmation itself should always come from the district.

Frequently Asked Questions

What district serves Goshen?

Goshen Community Schools, with nine schools including Goshen High School (home of the RedHawks), Goshen Junior High, Goshen Intermediate, Chandler Innovation Academy, and five elementary schools.

What is Goshen Community Schools' enrollment?

Roughly 6,200–6,500 students depending on the school year.

Where can families find current enrollment boundaries?

The district's own website, goshenschools.org — the best source for current boundaries and program details.
