Elkhart, Indiana School Districts

An Elkhart mailing address tells you less than you'd think about which school district a home is assigned to. Three separate public districts serve different sections of the city.

Elkhart Community Schools

The largest of the three, covering most of the city and the more populated northwest part of the county. It includes Elkhart High School and its Freshman Division, three middle schools, and thirteen elementary schools.

Concord Community Schools

Serves the southeast side of the city — and its boundary reaches into northwest Goshen as well. Dunlap sits between the two cities, and the district line doesn't follow the city line, so a Concord-assigned home could carry either an Elkhart or a Goshen mailing address.

Baugo Community Schools

Based just outside the city at 29125 County Road 22 West, Baugo serves the southwest side of Elkhart and part of the west-central county, feeding Jimtown Elementary, Intermediate, Junior High, and High School. This one is easy to miss — Baugo doesn't publish a public boundary map, but it's a real, active assignment for families with Elkhart addresses.

How to actually confirm which district applies

None of the three districts' boundaries can be reliably inferred from a mailing address or a general sense of "which side of town." Bus service adds another layer, since it isn't guaranteed for every address even within an assigned district, and routes shift year to year.

The only reliable method: confirm directly with the specific district's enrollment office before writing an offer, for both attendance assignment and bus eligibility. Lisa can identify which of the three boundaries a given address falls in and point you to the right office — but the confirmation itself should always come from the district.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many school districts serve Elkhart, Indiana?

Three — Elkhart Community Schools, Concord Community Schools, and Baugo Community Schools — each covering different sections of the city.

Does an Elkhart mailing address tell me which district a home is in?

No. The district lines don't follow city or postal boundaries, so the assignment depends on the specific address rather than the city name.

How do I confirm the district and bus service for a specific address?

Through that district's own enrollment office — the confirmation should come from the district rather than an agent or a website. Lisa will confirm which of the three boundaries an address falls in before you write an offer.
