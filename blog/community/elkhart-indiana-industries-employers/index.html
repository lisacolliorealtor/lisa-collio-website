Elkhart, Indiana Industries and Employers

Elkhart's economy has a clear center of gravity, but it's more diversified than the city's nickname suggests.

Recreational vehicles

RV manufacturing dominates the regional economy. Elkhart has been known as the RV Capital of the World since the late 1940s, and most of the industry's leading brands operate here or within a short drive — the Elkhart–Middlebury–Goshen corridor produces the great majority of North America's RVs. The industry's supply chain — parts suppliers, custom fabricators, and component manufacturers — adds a substantial second layer of employment beyond the RV brands themselves.

What else is here

Healthcare, education, logistics, and advanced manufacturing round out the picture. Elkhart's rail yards, among the largest freight classification yards in the world, still anchor a meaningful logistics sector on their own. The city's history in musical instrument manufacturing (led historically by Charles G. Conn) and pharmaceuticals (Miles Medical Company, later absorbed into Bayer) shows an industrial base that's always been broader than any single label.

The honest trade-off

The RV industry's strength is also a form of exposure. It's genuinely cyclical, and Elkhart felt that directly during the 2008–2009 downturn, when unemployment briefly spiked into national headlines. The rate has stayed below the national average since 2013, but anyone whose household income would depend entirely on the RV sector should factor that cyclicality into their planning. More detail specifically for RV industry buyers is in Buying a Home in Elkhart on an RV Industry Job.

Commuting for work

Many residents also commute to South Bend, Mishawaka, and Goshen, all within about 20 minutes — worth knowing if your job search isn't limited to Elkhart itself.

Frequently Asked Questions

What industry dominates Elkhart's economy?

Recreational vehicle manufacturing — Elkhart has been known as the RV Capital of the World since the late 1940s, and the Elkhart–Middlebury–Goshen corridor produces the great majority of North America's RVs.

What other industries are significant in Elkhart besides RVs?

Healthcare, education, logistics, and advanced manufacturing, plus a historic base in musical instrument manufacturing (led by Charles G. Conn) and pharmaceuticals (Miles Medical Company, later absorbed into Bayer). Elkhart's rail yards are also among the largest freight classification yards in the world.

Is the RV industry's dominance something to factor into a move here?

Worth knowing — it's genuinely cyclical, and Elkhart felt that directly during the 2008–2009 downturn. The rate has stayed below the national average since 2013, but it's worth factoring in if your household income would depend entirely on the sector.
