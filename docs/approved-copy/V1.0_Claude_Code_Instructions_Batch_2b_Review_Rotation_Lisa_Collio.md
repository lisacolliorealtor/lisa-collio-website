## 4. Rotation algorithm (per Build Plan Batch 2b spec)

Every page in the rotation displays exactly three review-related elements:
1. **One review with a matched photo** (from the eligible pool for that page's language, per Section 2)
2. **One review without a photo**
3. **One standalone client-general photo** (from the existing `assets/images/client-general/` pool — not tied to a specific named reviewer; inventory the existing folder rather than assuming a fixed list, since new client-general photos may have been added since the last batch)

**No-repeat rule:** no review may repeat on the same page (obviously) or on **adjacent pages** — defined as: pages linked directly from one another in primary navigation, and a page and its immediate parent/child in the site hierarchy (e.g., `/es/viviendo-en-goshen/` and its five linked FAQ articles count as adjacent to each other and to `/es/` itself). Two pages that only share a distant nav relationship (e.g., a Goshen article and an Elkhart article) are not considered adjacent and may repeat if needed — but prefer maximum spread across the full 55-eligible-review pool (62 total minus the 4 excluded minus 1 held plus back to accounting: actually confirm exact final eligible count once exclusions are applied) before any repeats occur at all.
