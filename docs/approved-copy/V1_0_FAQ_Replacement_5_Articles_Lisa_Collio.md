## `/blog/community/elkhart-indiana-industries-employers/`

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What industry dominates Elkhart's economy?
Recreational vehicle manufacturing — Elkhart has been known as the RV Capital of the World since the late 1940s, and the Elkhart–Middlebury–Goshen corridor produces the great majority of North America's RVs.

### What other industries are significant in Elkhart besides RVs?
Healthcare, education, logistics, and advanced manufacturing, plus a historic base in musical instrument manufacturing (led by Charles G. Conn) and pharmaceuticals (Miles Medical Company, later absorbed into Bayer). Elkhart's rail yards are also among the largest freight classification yards in the world.

### Is the RV industry's dominance something to factor into a move here?
Worth knowing — it's genuinely cyclical, and Elkhart felt that directly during the 2008–2009 downturn. The rate has stayed below the national average since 2013, but it's worth factoring in if your household income would depend entirely on the sector.

## `/blog/community/what-is-elkhart-indiana-known-for/`

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is Elkhart called the RV Capital of the World?
It's held the nickname since the late 1940s — the Elkhart–Middlebury–Goshen corridor is commonly cited as producing the great majority of North America's recreational vehicles, and most of the industry's leading brands are headquartered here or within a short drive.

### What is Elkhart known for besides RVs?
It was also once called the "Band Instrument Capital of the World" — Charles G. Conn began manufacturing band instruments here in 1874. Separately, Miles Medical Company, founded in Elkhart in 1884, invented Alka-Seltzer and Flintstones Vitamins before being absorbed into Bayer.

### Is Elkhart's downtown historically recognized?
Yes — the downtown commercial district and the State and Division Streets area are both on the National Register of Historic Places, and the city earned Preserve America Community status in 2008.

## `/blog/community/goshen-indiana-landmarks-amenities/`

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many Goshen properties are on the National Register of Historic Places?
Six: the Goshen Historic District itself, the Elkhart County Courthouse, the Fort Wayne Street Bridge, the Goshen Carnegie Public Library, the William N. Violett House, and the Violett-Martin House and Gardens.

### What's the best-known outdoor asset in Goshen?
The Pumpkinvine Nature Trail, a paved rail-trail running northeast out of town toward Middlebury and Shipshewana.

### What Elkhart attractions are close enough for Goshen residents to use regularly?
Wellfield Botanic Gardens and the Lerner Theatre, both in neighboring Elkhart.

## `/blog/community/elkhart-indiana-landmarks-amenities/`

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What historic sites are in Elkhart?
The downtown commercial district and the State and Division Streets area, both on the National Register of Historic Places, plus the Ruthmere Museum and adjoining Havilah Beardsley House.

### How big is Wellfield Botanic Gardens?
36 acres, with more than 20 themed gardens.

### Where is the RV/MH Hall of Fame & Museum located?
Along the toll road — it documents the history of the industry that gave Elkhart its nickname.

## `/blog/community/what-is-goshen-indiana-known-for/`

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What's the most visible thing about Goshen?
Its Historic District — about 45 city blocks of storefronts still in daily commercial use, which helped earn Goshen the Indiana Chamber of Commerce's Community of the Year in 2017. Locals call it the Maple City.

### What college is in Goshen?
Goshen College, a private liberal arts college that anchors both the local economy and a meaningful part of the community's character.

### What RV manufacturers operate near Goshen?
Thor Industries is headquartered in Elkhart, with Forest River, Keystone RV, Lippert Components, and Jayco all operating within a short drive of Goshen.
