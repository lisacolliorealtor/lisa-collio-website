# ARTICLE 1: What Goshen, Indiana Is Known For

---

Ask someone from the area what Goshen is known for, and the answer usually starts with its downtown — for good reason.

## A downtown that never got hollowed out

Goshen's Historic District, established in 1983, covers about 45 city blocks of storefronts dating to the late 1800s and early 1900s — still in daily commercial use, not preserved as a museum piece. In 2017, the Indiana Chamber of Commerce named Goshen the state's Community of the Year, largely on the strength of that downtown. Locals call it the Maple City.

## Its place in the RV corridor

Goshen sits inside the Elkhart–Middlebury–Goshen corridor, commonly cited as producing the great majority of North America's recreational vehicles. Thor Industries is headquartered in Elkhart, with Forest River, Keystone RV, Lippert Components, and Jayco all operating within a short drive of Goshen itself.

## Goshen College

The city is also home to Goshen College, a private liberal arts college that anchors both the local economy and a meaningful part of the community's character.

## The historic architecture underneath it all

Six Goshen properties sit on the National Register of Historic Places, including the Elkhart County Courthouse and the Goshen Carnegie Public Library — both downtown, both worth finding on a walk through the district.

For more on what that downtown actually looks like day to day, see [Downtown Goshen, Indiana](/blog/community/downtown-goshen-indiana/).

Most homes in Goshen fall under a single district, but not all of them — and the exceptions are easy to miss.

## Goshen Community Schools

The primary district, with nine schools serving roughly 6,200 to 6,500 students: Goshen High School (home of the RedHawks), Goshen Junior High, Goshen Intermediate, Chandler Innovation Academy, and five elementary schools. Private and parochial options are also available in the area, and Goshen College serves the community at the post-secondary level.

## Where it gets more complicated

Small portions of the city fall within Middlebury Community Schools, Concord Community Schools, or WaNee Community Schools instead of Goshen Community Schools. A Goshen mailing address does not guarantee a Goshen Community Schools assignment — the boundaries follow district lines, not city limits.

## How to confirm which district applies

The only reliable method is to confirm directly with the applicable district's enrollment office before writing an offer — for both attendance assignment and, separately, bus eligibility, since that isn't guaranteed for every address even within an assigned district. Lisa can help identify which boundary a specific address falls in and point you to the right office, but the confirmation itself should always come from the district.

Downtown Goshen is compact by design, not by accident, and that compactness is a big part of why it works.

## A dense, closely clustered district

Independent shops, restaurants, and coffee places sit close together rather than spread along a strip — the restored 600-seat **Goshen Theater**, the **Old Bag Factory**'s working studios, and a Designated Outdoor Refreshment Area covering much of the core all sit within easy reach of each other. **First Fridays**, a monthly downtown event running since 2007, is the clearest window into the district if you're visiting to get a feel for it.

## What anchors it

The **Goshen Theater** at 216 S. Main Street opened in 1905, survived a fire and a decades-long stretch as a church, and was restored by a nonprofit in 2014. It now runs a full calendar of live music, theater, dance, and film. The **Old Bag Factory**, a converted industrial building north of downtown, houses working studios rather than retail with a theme — you can often watch pieces being made. The **Goshen Farmers Market** runs year-round.

## Historic recognition

The Goshen Historic District itself, along with the Elkhart County Courthouse at its center, is on the National Register of Historic Places, alongside four other listed properties nearby.

## What it means for living nearby

Downtown proximity means shorter trips for dining, First Fridays, and the farmers market — a real consideration for buyers weighing an older, closer-in home against newer construction farther out.

---

Goshen's economy runs on the same regional engine as the rest of Elkhart County, with a few employers specific to the city itself.

## Recreational vehicles

The Elkhart–Middlebury–Goshen corridor is commonly cited as producing the great majority of North America's RVs. Thor Industries is headquartered in Elkhart; Forest River, Keystone RV, Lippert Components, and Jayco all operate within a short drive of Goshen. A significant share of buyers relocating to Goshen are coming for a job in that industry or its supply chain.

## Beyond RVs

The largest local employers outside the RV sector include IU Health Goshen Hospital, Goshen Community Schools, and Goshen College. Manufacturing outside the RV industry, healthcare, construction, and agriculture round out the picture, and commuting to Elkhart, Middlebury, Mishawaka, or South Bend is straightforward for residents whose work takes them outside the city.

## The honest trade-off

The same RV-industry strength that drives the regional economy is also a form of exposure — it's genuinely cyclical, and the region felt that directly during the 2008–2009 downturn. For more detail specific to RV industry buyers, see [Working in the RV Industry and Buying a Home in Goshen, Indiana](/blog/community/working-in-the-rv-industry-and-buying-a-home-in-goshen-indiana/).

Goshen's landmarks lean historic, and its amenities lean outdoorsy — a combination that shapes a lot of what living here actually feels like.

## Historic landmarks

Six Goshen properties sit on the National Register of Historic Places: the Goshen Historic District itself, the Elkhart County Courthouse at its center, the Fort Wayne Street Bridge, the Goshen Carnegie Public Library, the William N. Violett House, and the Violett-Martin House and Gardens. The **Old Bag Factory**, a converted industrial building turned working studio space, is a working landmark rather than a static one.

## Trails and parks

The **Pumpkinvine Nature Trail**, a paved rail-trail running northeast out of town toward Middlebury and Shipshewana, is the best-known outdoor asset in the area. Inside the city, **Fidler Pond Park** and **Abshire Park** provide closer-in green space, and **Ox Bow Park** is a short drive away along the Elkhart River. The Millrace and Maple City Greenway trails add further paved routes through the city.

## Nearby, in Elkhart

Wellfield Botanic Gardens and the Lerner Theatre, both in neighboring Elkhart, are close enough to count as regular options for Goshen residents — worth knowing if you're weighing the two cities against each other. See [Living in Elkhart, Indiana](/living-in-elkhart/) for more.

For a fuller sense of how these landmarks fit into an actual day or weekend, see [Things to Do in Goshen, Indiana](/blog/community/things-to-do-in-goshen-indiana/).
