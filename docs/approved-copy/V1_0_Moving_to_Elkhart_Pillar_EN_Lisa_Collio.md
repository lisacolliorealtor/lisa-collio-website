# Moving to Elkhart, Indiana: What to Know Before You Move

Elkhart is a city of roughly 54,000 people at the confluence of the St. Joseph and Elkhart Rivers, about 15 miles east of South Bend. Most people considering a move here are weighing the same three questions: what housing actually costs, where the work is, and whether daily life will fit. This page answers those directly — including the parts that aren't a sales pitch.

Lisa Collio has spent her career helping buyers and sellers in Goshen and Elkhart, Indiana, and the most useful thing she can offer someone who hasn't lived here yet isn't a list of amenities. It's context. Here's what she tells people who call from out of state.

## Where Elkhart sits, and what that means for getting around

Elkhart is the largest city in the Elkhart–Goshen metropolitan area, part of the broader South Bend–Elkhart–Mishawaka region — the area locals call Michiana. South Bend is about 20 minutes away; Goshen, the county seat, is another 20 minutes south. Chicago is roughly two hours by the Indiana Toll Road, which runs directly through the city with exits at 92 and 96 — worth knowing that Elkhart runs on Eastern time while Chicago runs on Central, which quietly costs you an hour heading west and buys one back on the return.

US-20 (the St. Joseph Valley Parkway) bypasses the south side of the city; State Road 19 runs through it; US-33 terminates here. Amtrak's Capitol Limited and Lake Shore Limited both stop at the Elkhart station, and the Interurban Trolley — a regional public bus service — connects Elkhart to Goshen, Dunlap, Osceola, and Mishawaka daily except Sundays and major holidays. South Bend International Airport, the nearest commercial service, is about 30 minutes away.

For most residents, the commute story is simple: it's short, and there are more ways to get around than the car alone.

## Where people work

The regional economy has a center of gravity, and it's recreational vehicles. Elkhart has been known as the RV Capital of the World since the late 1940s, and most of the industry's leading brands are headquartered here or within a short drive — the Elkhart–Middlebury–Goshen corridor is commonly cited as producing the great majority of North America's RVs. A significant share of buyers relocating to Elkhart are coming for a job in that industry or its supply chain.

Elkhart's economy is more diversified than the RV nickname suggests, though. The city also has a long history in musical instrument manufacturing — Charles G. Conn began making band instruments here in 1874, and Elkhart was once known as the "Band Instrument Capital of the World" alongside its RV title. Pharmaceuticals had deep roots here too: Miles Medical Company, founded in Elkhart in 1884, invented Alka-Seltzer and Flintstones Vitamins before the operation was absorbed into Bayer. Healthcare, logistics, education, and advanced manufacturing round out the picture today, and Elkhart's rail yards — among the largest freight classification yards in the world — still anchor a meaningful logistics sector.

Worth knowing honestly: the RV industry's strength is also a form of exposure. It's cyclical, and Elkhart felt that directly during the 2008–2009 downturn, when the city's unemployment rate briefly spiked into national headlines. The rate has stayed below the national average since 2013, but if your household income would depend entirely on that sector, that's a conversation worth having with a financial professional before you commit to a mortgage payment.

## Schools

Three public school districts serve sections of Elkhart, and which one applies to a given address is not something a city or county boundary tells you on its own.

**Elkhart Community Schools** is the largest, covering most of the city and the more populated northwest part of the county — Elkhart High School and its Freshman Division, three middle schools, and fourteen elementary schools.

**Concord Community Schools** serves the southeast side of the city, and its boundary also reaches into northwest Goshen — Dunlap sits between the two cities, and the district line doesn't follow the city line.

**Baugo Community Schools**, based just outside the city at 29125 County Road 22 West, serves the southwest side of Elkhart and part of the west-central county, feeding Jimtown Elementary, Intermediate, Junior High, and High School. This is easy to miss, because Baugo's own materials don't publish a public boundary map — but it's real: families with Elkhart mailing addresses are assigned to Jimtown schools every year.

An Elkhart mailing address, in other words, tells you almost nothing about which of the three districts an address falls in. Bus service adds another layer — it isn't guaranteed for every address even within an assigned district, and routes shift year to year.

Lisa doesn't rank schools or steer anyone toward a particular attendance area, and no agent should. What she will do is confirm which of the three boundaries a given address actually falls in before you write an offer, and point you to that district's own enrollment office to confirm both the assignment and bus eligibility directly — that confirmation should always come from the district, not from an agent or a website.

## The housing stock

Elkhart's housing tells the story of a city that grew in distinct waves. Near the center and along the historic corridors, you'll find late-1800s and early-1900s homes with genuine character — and mechanicals, windows, and foundations that deserve a careful inspection. Move outward and you hit mid-century construction on larger lots, then post-2000 subdivisions and current new construction toward the edges of the city and along the main commercial corridors. Riverfront parcels near the St. Joseph and Elkhart Rivers carry their own premium for the setting.

That range is genuinely useful, because it means the same budget can buy very different things depending on what you prioritize. Two buyers with identical pre-approvals routinely end up in completely different parts of the city.

**What none of this tells you is price**, because price moves. Current figures — median sale price, days on market, inventory — live on Lisa's [market data page](/market-stats/), sourced from the local MLS and updated quarterly. Anything you read about Elkhart home prices in a relocation article without a date on it is worth ignoring.

## What it costs to live here

Housing is the headline: it costs meaningfully less in Elkhart than in most of the country, and less than in the metro areas many transplants are leaving. Utilities and commuting costs tend to run lower too, largely because the drives are shorter.

Indiana's property tax structure includes a homestead deduction and constitutional caps on the rate applied to owner-occupied homes, which makes annual carrying costs more predictable than in a number of other states. Rates and assessments vary by parcel, so confirm the specific number with the Elkhart County Assessor's office before you build it into a budget rather than relying on a rule of thumb.

Winter heating is the line item people from warmer states underestimate. Ask for a year of utility history on any home you're serious about — sellers can usually provide it.

## The honest trade-offs

Every relocation guide has a "cons" section that lists nothing anyone would actually mind. Here are the real ones.

**Public transportation covers the basics, not everything.** The Interurban Trolley connects Elkhart to Goshen, Dunlap, Osceola, and Mishawaka, with onward connections toward South Bend — but the stops are limited, and Elkhart isn't a place you'd plan a daily life around transit alone. If you're coming from a city where you got by without a car, budget for one here.

**Winter is a real season.** Snow, gray stretches, and lake-effect systems rolling in off Lake Michigan. Some people love it; some spend three Februaries here and start looking south. Know which one you are before you buy, not after.

**The job market is concentrated.** RV manufacturing's strength is also its exposure — the industry is cyclical, and the region feels it when demand softens. See the note above on the 2008–2009 downturn.

**It's a small city, not a metro.** Fewer late-night options, fewer specialty retailers, a shorter restaurant list than a much larger city offers. Most residents find the trade worth making. Not everyone does.

## Elkhart or Goshen?

People relocating to the area often assume they'll choose a city first and a house second. In practice it usually runs the other way. Elkhart is the larger of the two, closer to the Toll Road, and built around its own downtown centered on the Lerner Theatre and Wellfield Botanic Gardens. Goshen is smaller and denser at the center, with a compact downtown business district of its own.

The practical advice: tour in both, and pay attention to the drive to wherever you'll actually be working. Twelve minutes versus thirty-five changes daily life more than a countertop does. Lisa works throughout both cities and has no reason to push you toward either one. More on the other option is on the [Moving to Goshen page](/moving-to-goshen/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the cost of living like in Elkhart, Indiana?

Housing is meaningfully below the national average, and utilities and commuting tend to run lower too, since drives are short. Winter heating is the cost transplants most often underestimate. Current figures live on Lisa's [market data page](/market-stats/).

### Which school district serves a given Elkhart address?

It depends on the address, not the city. Three districts — Elkhart Community Schools, Concord Community Schools, and Baugo Community Schools — each cover different sections of the city, and boundaries don't follow mailing addresses. Lisa confirms the assigned district before you write an offer and directs you to that district's enrollment office to confirm bus eligibility.

### Is Elkhart or Goshen the better place to relocate to?

It depends more on where you'll be working than on the city itself. Elkhart is larger and closer to the Toll Road; Goshen is smaller and denser at the center. Touring both and timing the actual commute tells you more than any list of amenities. See the [Elkhart community page](/living-in-elkhart/) for more on daily life here.

### What industries should I know about before moving to Elkhart for work?

RV manufacturing dominates the regional economy and is genuinely cyclical — worth a conversation with a financial professional if your household income depends entirely on it. Healthcare, logistics, education, and advanced manufacturing add real diversification beyond the RV nickname.

### How do I move to Elkhart from another state?

Sequence matters more than speed: the financing conversation first, a scouting visit second, an offer third — and there are a couple of steps most people do out of order. A full step-by-step is coming as its own article; in the meantime, [Lisa is glad to walk through it directly](/contact/).
