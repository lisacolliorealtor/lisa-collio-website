## "maple", not "arce" (Lisa, 29 July 2026)

Goshen's nickname is **the Maple City**, and Spanish copy uses **"maple"** —
never "arce" — for the tree and the leaves.

Two reasons: "maple" is the common usage in US Spanish, and it preserves the
tie to the city's own nickname, which appears untranslated as *the Maple City*
in Spanish copy and in the carved city emblem photographed for
`goshen-living-faq-known-for.jpg`.

**Do not change "hojas de maple" to "hojas de arce."** Verified 29 July 2026:
"arce" appears nowhere in Spanish copy, so the site is already consistent.
Apply this choice anywhere maple appears in future Spanish copy.

## Register

Spanish pages address the reader as **usted**, not tú. This holds in body copy,
CTAs, FAQ answers, and alt text alike.

## Title case

Title Case is correct for English pages and for Spanish page and legal titles.
Spanish **article** titles are sentence case. (Recorded in CLAUDE.md v1.4.)

## Middle schools

Neighbouring Spanish pages use **"intermedia"** rather than "secundaria" for
middle schools. Check adjacent pages before introducing a variant.

## Alt text

Spanish alt text is written natively from the photograph, not translated from
the English alt text, and must carry **equal descriptive detail** to its English
twin — a Spanish screen-reader user should receive the same information as an
English one. Alt text is copy: it passes through Lisa's review gate and is
subject to the Fair Housing terms list
(`content/source/fair-housing-terms.txt`, enforced by `audit.js` check 17).
