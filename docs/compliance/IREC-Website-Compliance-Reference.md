**For use by Claude Code when building/editing lisacolliorealtor.com and drafting articles**
Governing law: Indiana Code 25-34.1 (Real Estate License Law) + 876 IAC (Indiana Real Estate Commission rules) + IC 22-9.5 (Indiana Fair Housing Act)

> Compiled from official Indiana Administrative Code text (via Cornell LII / Justia mirrors of iar.iga.in.gov) as of July 2026. The IAC updates quarterly — re-verify against https://iar.iga.in.gov/code/current/876 before relying on this for a licensing/disciplinary matter. This is a working reference, not legal advice.

### Key Terms (IC 25-34.1-1-2)
- **Broker company** — the licensed business entity (RE/MAX Results). Distinct from any individual broker or team name.
- **Broker** — an individual who, for consideration, sells/buys/leases/manages/lists/appraises real estate, and who acts *in association with and under the auspices of* a managing broker and broker company. A broker is never legally independent of the broker company.
- **Managing broker** — the individual the Commission holds responsible for the actions of everyone affiliated with the broker company; the person whose sign-off matters for content review (see Section 4).
- **Licensee** — anyone holding a valid, unexpired license under this article.

## 1. Advertising Requirements — 876 IAC 8-1-8
*(This is the core rule governing the website, listing pages, social posts, and any paid ads.)*

- **(a)** Any display, classified advertising, sign, **Internet advertising**, or business card that carries a broker's name **must contain the name of the broker company** the broker is associated with, and the broker company's name must be **clearly visible**. All advertising must be under the direct supervision and in the name of the broker company (RE/MAX Results / The Viruez Team).
- **(b)** Advertising must reveal the broker company's name either exactly as licensed with the Commission, or by its publicly known name.
- **(c)** Internet, TV, or radio ads carrying a broker's name must carry the broker company name the same way. **Exception:** for character-limited displays (thumbnails, text messages, tweets/social posts), the broker company name can be omitted *only if* the post links through to a page that does show it.
- **(d)** Do **not** advertise in a way that implies the property is being offered by a private party not in the real estate business.
- **(e)** Do **not** run advertising showing only a PO box, phone number, or street address with no broker company identification.
- **(f)** Never place a sign, advertise, or list a property for sale/lease/rent without the **written consent of the owner** or their authorized agent.

**Practical application for the site:**
- Every page footer/header should show "RE/MAX Results" (or "The Viruez Team, RE/MAX Results") clearly, not buried.
- Individual agent bio pages, blog posts, and social embeds must not stand alone without the brokerage name visible or one click away.
- Never publish a listing, market update, or "coming soon" post about a specific property without confirmed written seller authorization.
- No page should read as if Lisa is selling/listing as a private individual — always "Lisa Collio, RE/MAX Results."

## 2. Prohibited Conduct / "Incompetent Practice" — 876 IAC 8-2-7
Selected items relevant to web copy, blog content, and testimonials:
- **(13)** Committing any act of **fraud or material deception** while performing licensed activities — i.e., no exaggerated/false claims in copy ("guaranteed sale," "#1 in Indiana" without substantiation, fabricated stats).
- **(14)** Any other violation of IC 25-34.1 or 876 IAC generally sweeps in unlicensed practice, false claims, etc.
- **(15)** A finalized discriminatory practice finding under the Indiana Civil Rights Act (IC 22-9) is itself a license violation — ties directly into Fair Housing rules below.

**Practical application:**
- No unverifiable superlatives or fabricated statistics/testimonials in copy Claude Code generates.
- No content implying outcomes ("we'll sell your home for X% over asking") that aren't factual/typical-result disclaimed.

## 3. Fair Housing Compliance — IC 22-9.5 + federal FHA + 910 IAC 2
Applies to **every** listing description, blog post, photo caption, and ad on the site.
- Protected classes under Indiana law: **race, color, religion, sex, disability/handicap, familial status, national origin, ancestry.**
- It is unlawful to publish, post, or mail any notice, statement, or advertisement that indicates a preference, limitation, or discrimination based on a protected class (IC 22-9.5-5-2).
- Applies to listing remarks, neighborhood descriptions, and any "ideal for..." language.

**Practical application — content rules Claude Code must follow:**
- **Never** describe a property or neighborhood using protected-class-coded language: no "great for families," "walking distance to [specific church]," "quiet retirees welcome," "safe neighborhood" (steering language), "no wheelchair access" framed as a downside, "perfect starter home for young couples," etc.
- Describe the **property and its features**, not the type of person who should buy it ("4 bedrooms, fenced yard" ✅ vs. "perfect for a growing family" ⚠️).
- Avoid demographic or "who lives here" framing in neighborhood/market blog content.
- Photos/captions should not exclude or imply exclusion of any protected group.
- Consider adding an Equal Housing Opportunity logo/statement in the site footer (standard industry practice, reinforces compliance).

## 4. Unlicensed Assistant / Content Review Rule — 876 IAC 8-1-8, IC 25-34.1-3-2
- An **unlicensed assistant may not** prepare promotional materials or advertisements without review and approval of the supervising/managing broker.
- Unlicensed persons may not answer questions about listings beyond list price, address, basic features, or directions.

**Practical application:**
- If anyone other than a licensed broker (e.g., a VA or marketing assistant) is drafting site copy, it should be flagged for **managing broker review/approval before publishing** — note this as a workflow step, not something Claude Code can verify itself, but the site's content workflow should include a sign-off step.

---

## 5. Broker/Team Naming Rules (Indiana REALTORS legal guidance interpreting 876 IAC 8-1-8)
- Indiana **does not formally recognize "teams"** in license law. Any team branding (e.g., "The Viruez Team") must always be **supervised by the Managing Broker** and done in the **name of the broker company**.
- The broker company's name must be **equal or greater prominence** than any individual or team name in advertising — don't let "The Viruez Team" branding visually outweigh "RE/MAX Results" on any page.

---

## 6. Residential Real Estate Sales Disclosure — 876 IAC 9-1
- Governs the seller's disclosure form under IC 32-21-5-7. Not directly a website content issue, but if the site publishes any educational content *about* the disclosure form/process, it should describe it accurately and not give legal advice — link to the official form/IREC resources rather than reproducing legal interpretations as fact.

---

## 7. Quick Pre-Publish Checklist (for Claude Code to run against any new page/post)
- [ ] Does every page clearly show "RE/MAX Results" (brokerage name), not just "Lisa Collio" or "The Viruez Team" alone?
- [ ] Is the brokerage name equal or greater in prominence than any individual/team name?
- [ ] If a specific property is featured, is there confirmed written seller consent to advertise it?
- [ ] Does listing/neighborhood copy describe the **property**, not the type of buyer/renter who "belongs" there?
- [ ] Is any protected-class-coded language (family-friendly, walk to church, safe area, etc.) present? If so, rewrite around concrete features/amenities instead.
- [ ] Are any claims/statistics ("top agent," "sells homes X% faster") verifiable and non-misleading?
- [ ] Is contact info more than just a bare PO box/phone/address with no brokerage identification?
- [ ] Was this content reviewed/approved by the managing broker if drafted by unlicensed support staff?
