# Verified Facts — canonical record

**Purpose.** Every factual claim that appears on more than one page lives here
once, with its source. Pages cite this file; this file cites the outside world.

**Why it exists.** The "13 vs 14 elementary schools" regression happened because
a correction landed on one page while an approved source document still carried
the old number, so the wrong figure kept regenerating on rebuild. Nothing caught
it because no single place held the right answer. Facts stated independently on
six pages will eventually disagree; facts stated once will not.

## How to use it

- **Writing.** No number, date, name, or count enters an article unless it is
  already in this file. If it is not here, verify it and add it *first*.
- **Correcting.** Fix the entry here first, then check every page listed under
  **Appears on** against this file — never against each other.
- **Adding a fact.** Record the value, the primary source, any cross-reference,
  and the pages that state it. A fact with no source does not go on the site.
- **Disagreeing sources.** Record the disagreement rather than silently picking a
  winner. Two entries below do exactly that.
- **Approved copy still governs.** This file is the factual record, not an
  approval. Changing a published figure still needs Lisa's sign-off, and market
  figures remain canonical only on /market-stats/.

Sources listed here are the same ones published on `/sources/` and `/es/fuentes/`.
Where an entry says "cross-reference", two independent sources agree — that is why
the figure is treated as confirmed rather than single-sourced.

## Schools

### Elkhart — districts serving the city
**Three:** Elkhart Community Schools, Concord Community Schools, Baugo Community
Schools. An Elkhart mailing address does not determine which applies; assignment
depends on the specific address.
· **Sources:** elkhart.k12.in.us · Wikipedia, "Concord Community Schools (Indiana)"
· baugo.org
· **Appears on:** /blog/community/elkhart-indiana-school-districts/ ·
/blog/spanish/distrito-escolar-elkhart-indiana/ · /moving-to-elkhart/ ·
/es/mudarse-a-elkhart/
· **Never** rank or recommend a district; route confirmation to that district's
own enrollment office.

### Elkhart Community Schools — composition
**Thirteen elementary schools**, three middle schools, Elkhart High School as a
two-campus system (Main Campus + separate Freshman Division), plus alternative and
career-center campuses. **Enrollment above 11,000.** Also serves Bristol and
Simonton Lake.
· **Primary:** elkhart.k12.in.us · **Cross-reference:** Wikipedia, "Elkhart
Community Schools", which lists the same thirteen by name — Beardsley, Mary Beck,
Bristol, Cleveland, Mary Daly, Eastwood, Mary Feeser, Monger, Osolo, Pinewood,
Riverview, Roosevelt, Woodland
· **Appears on:** the four pages above, in EN and ES
· ⚠️ **This is the figure that regressed.** It read "fourteen" on the rebuilt
English page and in two approved source docs while five other pages said thirteen.
Verified as **thirteen**. If a source doc says otherwise, the source doc is wrong.

### Baugo Community Schools
Based at **29125 County Road 22 West**, outside the city. Serves southwest Elkhart
and part of the west-central county, feeding **Jimtown Elementary, Intermediate,
Junior High, and High School** — four schools.
· **Source:** baugo.org
· ⚠️ **Baugo publishes no public boundary map.** No source is cited for its
boundary, and none should be implied. Address-level assignment comes from the
district's enrollment office only.

### Goshen Community Schools
**Nine schools**, including Goshen High School (RedHawks), Goshen Junior High,
Goshen Intermediate, Chandler Innovation Academy, and five elementary schools.
**Enrollment roughly 6,200–6,500**, depending on the school year.
· **Primary:** goshenschools.org · **Cross-reference:** Wikipedia, "Goshen High
School" · NCES district search (nces.ed.gov)
· **Appears on:** /blog/community/goshen-indiana-school-district/ ·
/blog/spanish/distrito-escolar-goshen-indiana/ · /living-in-goshen/ ·
/es/viviendo-en-goshen/
· **Caveat that must travel with it:** small portions of Goshen fall within
Middlebury, Concord, or WaNee Community Schools. Concord in particular reaches
into northern Goshen and southern Elkhart, because Dunlap sits between the two
cities and the district line does not follow the city line. A Goshen mailing
address does not guarantee a Goshen Community Schools assignment.

## Cities

### Elkhart — population and founding
**Roughly 54,000** residents (53,923). **Platted 1832**, at the junction of
established trails.
· **Sources:** Wikipedia, "Elkhart, Indiana" · Encyclopaedia Britannica ·
U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts
· ⚠️ **Recorded disagreement:** ACHP Preserve America gives **52,000** and
**1831**. Wikipedia and Britannica agree on the figures above, so the majority
values are published and the discrepancy is recorded rather than resolved
silently.

### Goshen — population and founding
About **34,000** residents. **Platted 1831**, named for the biblical Land of
Goshen. County seat of Elkhart County. Known locally as **the Maple City**.
· **Sources:** U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts · STATS Indiana · City of Goshen
· ⚠️ **Do not confuse with Elkhart's 1832.** Goshen 1831, Elkhart 1832 — two
cities, adjacent dates, and the single easiest error to make in this cluster.

### Elkhart — location
East of South Bend and Mishawaka, near the Indiana–Michigan state line. About
**25 minutes northeast of Goshen**. South Bend, Mishawaka, and Goshen are all
within roughly 20 minutes.
· **Sources:** Wikipedia, "Elkhart, Indiana" · STATS Indiana
· **Phrasing:** plain geography only. "Northern Indiana" is barred as a
service-area descriptor; the service area is "Goshen and Elkhart, Indiana".

## Economy

### The RV corridor
The **Elkhart–Middlebury–Goshen** corridor produces the great majority of North
America's recreational vehicles — **commonly cited as more than 80%** of global RV
production. Elkhart has been called the **RV Capital of the World since the late
1940s**.
· **Sources:** Wikipedia, "Thor Industries" · RV PRO · Lippert company blog ·
RV Industry Association (rvia.org)
· **Manufacturers:** Thor Industries (headquartered in Elkhart; owns Airstream,
Jayco, Heartland), Forest River, Keystone RV, Lippert Components
· **Note:** the rewritten English "known for" and "industries" articles
deliberately describe the industry **without naming manufacturers**. Check the
page's own approach before adding names.

### Cyclicality
The RV industry is genuinely cyclical. Elkhart's unemployment **spiked into
national headlines during the 2008–2009 downturn** and has **stayed below the
national average since 2013**.
· **Sources:** Wikipedia, "Elkhart, Indiana" · RV PRO
· **Required framing:** anyone whose household income would depend entirely on
the sector should discuss it with a financial professional. Never a lending or
qualification prediction.

### Elkhart beyond RVs
**Charles G. Conn began manufacturing band instruments in Elkhart in 1874**; the
city was once known as the **Band Instrument Capital of the World**. **Miles
Medical Company, founded in Elkhart in 1884**, invented Alka-Seltzer and
Flintstones Vitamins before being absorbed into Bayer. The city's rail yards, now
operated by **Norfolk Southern**, are among the **largest freight classification
yards in the world**.
· **Sources:** Wikipedia, "Elkhart, Indiana" · Encyclopaedia Britannica

### Goshen employers
**Goshen College** (private liberal arts) and **IU Health Goshen Hospital** are
the two standout local employers outside the RV industry.
· **Sources:** Goshen Community Schools "About Goshen" page · City of Goshen ·
Goshen Chamber of Commerce

### Other Elkhart museums
Ruthmere Museum and the adjoining Havilah Beardsley House · National New York
Central Railroad Museum · Midwest Museum of American Art (**more than 6,000
works**, Main Street) · Hall of Heroes Superhero Museum.
· **Sources:** Visit Elkhart County · Tripadvisor (cross-reference)

### Elkhart parks
**Island Park** at the confluence of the Elkhart and St. Joseph Rivers · the
Riverwalk · Studebaker, American, and McNaughton Parks, within a **35-facility**
park system.
· **Source:** City of Elkhart

### Goshen downtown
**The Goshen Theater**, 216 S. Main Street — **opened 1905**, restored by a
nonprofit in **2014**, **600 seats**. **The Old Bag Factory**, a converted
industrial building housing working studios. **First Fridays**, monthly since
**2007**. Year-round farmers market. A Designated Outdoor Refreshment Area covers
much of the core.
· **Sources:** City of Goshen · Goshen Chamber of Commerce
· **Phrasing:** describe by density and feature. **Never "walkable"** or any
ambulatory phrasing — Fair Housing. `npm run audit` fails on it.

### Historic designations
**Goshen — six NRHP properties:** the Goshen Historic District, the Elkhart County
Courthouse, the Fort Wayne Street Bridge, the Goshen Carnegie Public Library, the
William N. Violett House, and the Violett-Martin House and Gardens. The Historic
District runs about **45 city blocks** of storefronts still in daily commercial
use. Goshen was the Indiana Chamber of Commerce's **Community of the Year in
2017**.
**Elkhart:** the downtown commercial district and the State and Division Streets
area are both on the National Register; the city earned **Preserve America
Community status in 2008**.
· **Sources:** National Register of Historic Places · City of Goshen · ACHP
Preserve America

### Pumpkinvine Nature Trail
Paved rail-trail running northeast out of Goshen toward Middlebury and
Shipshewana.
· **Source:** City of Goshen
· **Phrasing:** describe as paved and feature-based, never in walking terms.
